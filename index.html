EXPEDITION GUIDE

🕹️ DUAL KEYBOARD CONTROLS (WASD & ARROW KEYS)

JUMP:

• Both WASD and Arrow Keys work interchangeably and simultaneously.
• ESC or P : Pause Game
✨ RESPONSIVE PHYSICS
• Coyote Time: You can jump for a brief moment even after stepping off a platform edge.
• Jump Buffering: Jump inputs registered right before touching down will trigger automatically on landing.
• Corner Slipping: Grazing ceiling corners gently nudges your character onto the platform.
💎 OBJECTIVES & COLLECTIBLES
• Coins & Gems: Earn bonus exploration points.
• Ancient Keys 🔑: Unlock sealed stone gates blocking your route.
• Sacred Relic 🏆: Every chamber requires claiming its Sacred Relic before the Exit Portal opens!
• Extra Life Heart ❤️: Restores +1 Life (max 6) to keep your expedition alive!
🚩 CHECKPOINTS & LIVES SYSTEM
• Touching an ancient totem activates a Checkpoint, saving your respawn location.
• Dying consumes 1 Life and respawns you with temporary invulnerability.
• If all lives reach 0, game is over and the run must restart from Level 1!
👾 COMBAT & HAZARDS
• Stomp patrolling crawlers and flying foes by landing on their heads from above.
• Dodge rhythmic flame jets, oscillating sawblades, and turret blasts!
